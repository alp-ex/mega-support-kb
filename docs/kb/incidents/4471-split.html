How to tell them apart

  • parser: the tenant writes the day first and the shared parser reads the month first, so day and month swap. It covers every channel except the booking-v3 form, for every tenant on file.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. Day-first people type what the hint says, and the form then reads it the tenant’s way, so day and month swap again. Only reports sent through that form, from tenants that run booking-v3, belong here. The parser fix does not reach it; the hint has to follow the tenant’s locale.
  • shift: the date moves by one day, not by a month. Weekdays slip (“Tuesday 6 October showed as Monday”), and one meeting showed on different days for people in Lisbon and Toronto. A swap of day and month cannot do that, so neither fix above closes it. The desk had already read these reports this way (desk-notes.json); the cause table still counts them under the tenant’s cause.

Why intake got it wrong

Intake tags a report from the reporter’s browser locale. The cause depends on the tenant: where they are and which booking form they run (locales.ts). The intake tagged column shows how far the two drift: reports from an en-US browser were tagged hint whatever form they came through.

Why it stayed one ticket
